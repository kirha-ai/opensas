Notation: K    = Number of Groups

                   ||  |Within SS Matrix(i)|                    
          V    = -----------------------------------            
                                          N/2

                       |       1        1   |  2P + 3P - 1      
          RHO  = 1.0 - | SUM -----  -  ---  | -------------     
                       |_     N(i)      N  _|  6(P+1)(K-1)      
                                                                
          DF   = .5(K-1)P(P+1)                                  
                                           _                  _ 
                                          |    PN/2            |
                                          |   N        V       |
